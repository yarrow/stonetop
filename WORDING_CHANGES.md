# Wording changes

I've changed the wording of a few passages from what's printed in the books to make things sound a little better in Apple VoiceOver.

## Setting Overview

### The village itself…

**As printed:**

> **…is home to around 300 folks.** It's a nice place, though poor. People look out for each other, here.

**In the app:**

> **…is home to around 300 folks.** It's a nice place, though poor. Here, people look out for each other.

**Why:** VoiceOver treats the comma as a full stop, so "here" sounds like the sentence
fragment "Here."

### The characters

**As printed:**

> - **The Heavy** (low/medium complexity): not just a violent individual—*our* violent individual. A champion, yes, but a bit of a liability, too.

**In the app:**

> - **The Heavy** (low/medium complexity): not just a violent individual—*Stonetop's* violent individual. A champion, yes, but a bit of a liability, too.

**Why:** VoiceOver doesn't do italics, so as printed this sounds like a run-on:

> not just a violent individual our violent individual

Changing "our" to "Stonetop's" makes the meaning clear without the emphasis.

### The Vicinity

**As printed:**

> **The Red Grove** is a stand of trees in the forest that turn blood red (leaves, bark, and all) near the autumnal equinox. But by springtime, they're back to normal.

**In the app:**

> **The Red Grove** is a stand of trees in the forest that turn blood red—leaves, bark, and all—near the autumnal equinox. But by springtime, they're back to normal.

**Why:** VoiceOver pauses at the opening parenthesis but not at the closing one. The dashes
give a pause in both places.
